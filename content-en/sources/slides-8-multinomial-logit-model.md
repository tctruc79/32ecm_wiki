## Role

Topic 8 slide deck — extends [[concepts/binary-response-models]] to a dependent variable with **more than 2 unordered (nominal) choices**. The theory (definitions, log-odds vs. the base category, probability formula, log-likelihood) extracted fully via text; the illustrative example (pp. ~15–42) is R output as images — the **McFadden R²** formula (p. 37, heavily used — see the concept page) was separately visually verified.

## Example data
